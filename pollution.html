Global warming is the gradual increase in Earth’s average temperature, mainly caused by human activities like burning fossil fuels (coal, oil, and gas). These activities release greenhouse gases, such as carbon dioxide and methane, into the atmosphere. These gases trap heat, leading to rising global temperatures.

As a result, we see serious effects like melting glaciers, rising sea levels, stronger storms, droughts, and disruptions to wildlife and ecosystems. Global warming also threatens food and water security for people around the world.

To fight it, we need to reduce emissions, switch to clean energy sources like solar and wind, and protect forests that absorb carbon dioxide. Everyone can help by using energy wisely, reducing waste, and supporting environmental policies.
