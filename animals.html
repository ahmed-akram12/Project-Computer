Climate change is affecting animals all over the world. As temperatures rise and habitats change, many animals are struggling to survive. Some species are losing their homes—like polar bears, whose sea ice is melting, or coral reef fish, whose reefs are dying from heat.

Animals that depend on certain weather patterns or seasons are getting confused. For example, birds might migrate too early or too late, and food might not be available when they arrive. This can lead to fewer babies being born or raised.

Climate change also causes more wildfires, floods, and droughts, which can destroy homes and reduce food sources. Some animals are trying to move to cooler places, but not all of them can.
