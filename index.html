Global Warming!!

Global warming has been a worldwide issue in the past years where thats the definition of it:

Global warming is the long-term rise in Earth's average temperature due to increased greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide from burning fossil fuels. This leads to melting ice, rising sea levels, and extreme weather. Its a major environmental issue that needs global action. It doesnt only affect humans but it can affect also animals living in water or earth and the same for plants.
