Climate change has a big impact on plants around the world. As temperatures rise and weather patterns shift, plants are struggling to adapt. Some species are growing earlier in the year or blooming too soon, which can affect pollination and survival.

Droughts, heatwaves, and stronger storms also damage plants and reduce their ability to grow. In some places, crops are producing less food, which affects farmers and food supply. Forests are also at risk—trees may die off or be replaced by different species that can handle warmer temperatures.

Additionally, climate change allows pests and diseases to spread more easily, hurting both wild and farmed plants.
